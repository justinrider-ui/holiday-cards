Write a Holiday Card for a Warrior

Couldn't make it to card night? You can still join in! Fill in the blanks, print your card, and decorate the front. Your card goes to a service member through the Holiday Cards for Our Military Challenge, which is based right here in New Hampshire.

  1. 1Fill in the blanks
  2. 2Print & fold
  3. 3Decorate & turn it in

Printing works best with this page in its own tab. Open the card maker in a new tab →

1About you

The Challenge asks for your first name only and your town. Little Scouts: tell a grown-up what to type, then draw the picture yourself!

2Pick your greeting

This goes in big letters at the top of your card.

3Fill in the blanks

Fill in as many as you like and skip the rest. Stuck? Tap Idea. Troops say the personal parts, like what your family does and what you love, are their favorite.

4Anything else?

Tell a joke, share something about you, or say it in your own words. (Skip questions, since they can't write back.)

Prints on one regular sheet of paper. Fold it in half, top to bottom, with the words inside. Then decorate the front!

Grown-up check

  • First name and town only. No last names, addresses, emails, or social media.
  • Kind and respectful. Skip politics or anything that might upset someone far from home.
  • Holiday cards only. The Challenge does not accept Veterans Day cards.
  • No envelope on the card. No return-address labels, either.

Turn it in

Bring it to the Pack: our den meeting on Thursday, November 12 at 6:30 PM, Moultonborough Function Hall. We'll send all of Pack 369's cards together.

Or mail it yourself to:

Laura Landerman-Garber

The Challenge's goal is to have cards in by November 15.

No printer?

No problem. Use any blank card or a sheet of paper folded in half (no bigger than 8½×11 folded), and copy your words in your own handwriting. Handwritten cards are great!

Start with "Dear Warrior," and sign with your first name, town, and "Cub Scout Pack 369."
